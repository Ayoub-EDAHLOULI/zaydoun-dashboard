Features

Everything Zaydoun can do with a book.

One reading partner, on the web and in your pocket. Here's what's in the box.

01

Answers you can check

Zaydoun finds the passages that matter in your PDF, answers from them, and tags the pages. Nothing pulled from somewhere else and passed off as the book.

  • Searches by meaning, not just matching words
  • “Page N” tag on every answer
  • Cited pages collected per conversation

02

Voice-first, hands-free

In the mobile app, say his name and talk. Your voice is transcribed, the answer is read back to you, and the whole exchange is saved to your account.

  • Wake word: “Zaydoun”
  • Spoken answers in your reply language
  • Voice on the web is on the way
03

Five languages, Arabic done right

Upload in English, Arabic, French, Spanish or Chinese, and choose the reply language for each conversation. Arabic reads right to left in a proper Arabic typeface.

English العربية Français Español 中文
04

PDF in, conversation out

Drop in a PDF up to 50 MB. Zaydoun splits it page by page and builds an index of what's where. You'll see each step as it happens.

Pending Processing Ready
05

A library that remembers

Your books on one shelf, every conversation saved and filterable by book. Reopen a thread from last month and pick up mid-argument.

06

An admin console for teams

Admins manage readers, roles and access, and see usage and cost by day and by conversation. Readers just see their books.

07

Private by design

Your books and conversations belong to your account alone, and you can delete any of it.
