Meet Zaydoun

Not a search box. A reading partner.

Sharp, warm and a little Moroccan. He'll tell you when an argument is weak, yours or the author's, and drop the odd word of Darija when the moment calls for it.

How it works

From PDF to conversation
in three steps.

No setup, no settings to learn. Bring the book you're already reading.

  1. 01Upload on the webDrop a PDF, add the title and its language. Arabic books welcome.
  2. 02Zaydoun reads itHe goes through it page by page and remembers where every idea lives.
  3. 03Talk, hands-freeType here on the web, or open the app, say “Zaydoun” and just speak.
