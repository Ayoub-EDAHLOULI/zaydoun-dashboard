Is ʿasabiyya just a fancy word for tribalism?
Zaydoun

Not quite, and that's the trap most summaries fall into. ʿAsabiyya is group feeling: the solidarity that lets people act as one and defend each other.

Kinship is where it starts, but Ibn Khaldun is clear that alliance and shared hardship can build it too. So it's less “my tribe first” and more the social glue that makes a group capable of founding a state. Wakha, here's the twist: the same glue is what wears out.
